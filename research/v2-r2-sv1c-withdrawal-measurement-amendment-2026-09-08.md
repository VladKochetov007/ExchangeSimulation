# SV1C withdrawal measurement amendment

Date: 2026-09-08
Candidate: V2-R2-SV1C-24H-CDF-LIQUIDITY-STRICT-RISK

SV1C inherits the finite-supplier withdrawal and counterfactual measurement
contract from the archived SV1B development checkpoint. The registered
interpretation is unchanged: a withdrawal, cancellation, or reprice must be
caused by the supplier's delayed local observation, inventory, risk, loss
budget, or an unavailable-side condition. A structural callback that guarantees
replacement liquidity is not a qualifying withdrawal.

This document is an SV1C provenance input. It authorizes no execution, does
not alter the predecessor evidence, and does not make a survival claim.
Supplier activity remains subject to the strict-risk amendment and its
independent activation review.
