## Exact corrections in the candidate

### Population-aware activation

`8f04a4f` makes the activation contract population-aware. Treatment cells
require the registered positive CDF roster, supplier decisions/fills,
inventory response, marked PnL exposure, bounded positions/quotes, and no
borrowing. Control cells require a valid zero-roster audit with zero CDF
activity and an empty supplier list. The control is represented as
`cdf_liquidity_activation_observed=false`; this is intentional and is not an
inactive treatment.

The lower inventory check now uses `min_position >= -configured_max_position`.
The old duplicated upper-bound check was an audit defect and is covered by a
mutation test.

### Typed price-unavailable audit

`c11ca73` adds a typed `priceunavailable` analyzer artifact over the canonical
`OrderRejected` event stream. It counts exact `PRICE_UNAVAILABLE` errors and
fails closed on malformed or missing rejection errors. The SV1 extractor and
scorer require the artifact and its zero malformed count. The old raw spot
JSONL grep remains only for historical evidence and is not used as the binary
SV1 oracle.

### Bounded delayed calendar listing

`0f874af` retains the accepted calendar and adds a registered 60-second maximum
delay for an option listing after its scheduled request. The first retained
startup listing was observed at scheduled time plus three seconds; later
calendar listings were on time. The contract still requires listing before
expiry and rejects a 61-second delayed mutation. This bounds automation/source
availability delay without encoding a price or survival result.

### Complete survival window contract

`d980df9` changes the survival contract to
`v2-r2-sv1-24h-survival-side-availability-v2`. After the explicit one-hour
warm-up, the scorer invokes `mvanalyze` with life-edge judgment enabled and
requires exactly 23 one-hour CDF/USD windows for each of the three registered
venues. It checks the expected starts, one-hour ends, venue set, book counts,
post-warm-up snapshots, aggregate two-sided share, and every-window side
availability. A missing, shifted, duplicated, or unexpected-venue window
cannot be hidden by an aggregate.

The transformation is in the shared
`scripts/v2-r2-sv1-survival-summary.jq` contract and is exercised by
`scripts/test-v2-r2-sv1-survival-contract.sh` with valid, missing-window,
shifted-window, and unexpected-venue fixtures. The scorer requires every
survival predicate to be true for treatment qualification. Control survival is
retained as a diagnostic; a control's expected failure is not converted into
an invalid treatment result. This endpoint disposition supersedes the
pre-cell v1 scoring wording and does not change the simulator.
