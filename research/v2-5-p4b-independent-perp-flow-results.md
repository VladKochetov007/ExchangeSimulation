# V2-5 P4b — independent perpetual-flow funding screen

Status: **FALSIFIED AT EXECUTION (development screening)**.  This is the
registered result of the two development pairs.  It is not a funding,
profitability, stability, liquidity, or realism claim.

## Question and registered decision

P4b held one already validated bounded physical-exposure hedger per venue
fixed in both arms and changed only the funding cap: A = 1 bps per funding
interval, B = 75 bps.  The preregistered chain was:

```text
delivered funding -> independent expected funding -> exact net carry
-> target inventory -> ordinary non-atomic spot/perpetual execution
-> paired basis response
```

The registered primary endpoint is the exact paired change in oriented
spot/perpetual premium.  Both development pairs had to pass the chain and
have a positive paired statistic for `SUPPORTED (screening)`; a target with no
matched ordinary fill is `FALSIFIED AT EXECUTION`.

## Interpretation fence

P4b establishes that the fixed independent perpetual-flow source and funding
intervention can activate the first four links, but the development evidence
does not identify a registered funding-driven basis response.  One seed fails
the required matched execution gate; the other executes and has an exact-zero
basis effect.  This is not evidence that funding is generally irrelevant, and
it does not license increasing capital, changing funding magnitude, changing
depth/spreads/clocks, or selecting a holdout after observing the result.

Untouched holdouts **419, 421, and 431 were not consumed**.  Because the
development predicate was not positive and fully complete in both pairs, no
holdout replication or exposure-off/on factorial is licensed by this protocol.
