# V2-6 option causal disposition — adversarial review

Review type: **fallback fresh second pass by the primary agent; not an
independent Sol-xhigh review**.  The available Sol-xhigh reviewer agents were
blocked by the model-usage limit.

## Verdict

**ACCEPT WITH NARROWER CLAIM.**

The disposition correctly closes the option-emergence and O2 directional
transmission questions as `NOT IDENTIFIED` under the existing protocol while
retaining P6-R1's valid viability/activation result.  It does not convert
explicit SABR/Vanna--Volga structure into emergence or consume another seed.

## Red-team checks

### Stage deltas

O0/O1/O2/O3/O4 are cumulative stage configurations, and the P6-R1 holdout
cells are valid.  O3−O2 and O4−O3 add explicit participant priors, so they are
useful activation contrasts but cannot establish endogenous smile structure.
O1−O0 is descriptive because no effect-size corridor or inferential rule was
registered.  O1→O2 is not a single mechanism: it changes liability demand and
dealer delta hedging together.

### Endpoint discipline

The P6 preregistration says that O2 directional transmission requires a
registered sign, but it does not fix that sign or a threshold.  The positive
observed correlations cannot therefore be promoted after seeing outcomes.
The disposition does not substitute an ATM/slope/curvature difference or a
dealer self-report for the missing primary endpoint.

### Information and measurement

The reviewed P6-R1 artifacts infer IV from persisted market prices and keep
Black--76 domain skips explicit.  Dealer and value-taker model volatility is
not used as market IV.  Receipt/frontier, lifecycle, conservation and evidence
digests are already independently checked for every valid cell.  No claim in
the disposition relies on a hidden index or an actor's own model value.

### Holdout and post hoc risks

Development seeds 211/213 and consumed R1 holdouts 223/227/229 are identified
by protocol.  The disposition does not consume a new holdout, choose a stage
after outcomes, add a threshold, or reinterpret the incomplete original P6
worlds as valid.  It explicitly treats R1's holdout result as viability and
activation only.

### Claim boundary

The proposed permitted claims are no stronger than the evidence: stage
viability/activation is screening support; SABR/VV structure is inherited; O2
activation is real but its directional effect is unresolved.  The document
prohibits emergence, realism, profitability and broader causal claims.

## Remaining limitation

The disposition is not a substitute for the independent Sol-xhigh review
requested by the research process.  If reviewer capacity becomes available,
the same preregistration, artifacts and this review should be sent for a true
second-party review before any final V2 autopsy.  Because no clean existing
single-mechanism contrast supports the desired emergence claim, this capacity
limitation does not justify a new outcome run or post-hoc option experiment.
