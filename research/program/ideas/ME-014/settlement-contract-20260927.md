## Why a separate settlement mark is needed

The draft defines 60 past venue-local spot/perp price pairs for the **rate**, but not the price used to value a position's notional when that rate becomes due. These are distinct economic objects. A live two-sided perp midpoint at `t⁻` would price current exposure, but withdrawing one quote immediately before `t` would make the entire payment unavailable; a tiny boundary quote could move every account's charge. A permanently external index would be a different, explicitly exogenous benchmark ecology and cannot be imported as an invisible rescue for a thin local book. A cached last-valid midpoint after a side disappears is not a current book state.

For this *research* candidate, choose the arithmetic mean of the same 60 valid prior **perp** midpoint samples as the funding-notional mark. The registered premium remains the **mean of 60 per-sample perp/spot ratios**, not a ratio of mean prices. The mean settlement mark is not an executable closeout price and must never substitute for a risk mark, terminal valuation, or actor-visible instantaneous quote. It is a lagged, publicly predictable benchmark that participants may influence over the minute. This manipulation and selection risk is a limitation to measure, not evidence of real-exchange fidelity. Binance's [funding explanation](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/360033525031) supports the general mark-times-position notional and opposite-side transfer, but its actual premium/index construction uses impact depth and an external spot index. Those features are **not** present in this E2 convention. Deribit's [funding specification](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/31424939178397-Funding-Specifications) describes continuous funding, not this scheduled compressed mechanism.

## Exact candidate rule

For venue `v` and scheduled instant `t`, the only eligible rate/mark inputs are its own 60 pairs `s=t−60 s, …, t−1 s`, sampled once per simulated second from live, positive, uncrossed, two-sided **spot and perp** books. The bid and ask are the best **displayed public** levels, not hidden-only executable levels; hidden reserve contributes no displayed depth. If a hidden-only best price masks a deeper displayed level, use that deeper public level. This explicitly narrows the E2 draft's "executable-book midpoint" shorthand: a hidden order may be executable but is not a public funding benchmark quote. A displayed resting quote may be old but must still be live at sampling. The sample captures the book after all work strictly before `s` and before any work stamped `s`; no same-time order can retroactively alter it. The production sampler must prove venue, symbol, best-displayed bid/best-displayed ask, depth, quote age, sample timestamp and causal event order. The current `FundingWindowSample` value carries only prices/timestamps and cannot prove provenance on its own. A **canonical observation of an unavailable book** (absent side, one-sided, non-positive or crossed displayed pair) makes the scheduled attempt **RATE_UNAVAILABLE**; no zero rate, interpolation, cached mark or alternate price is allowed. Record the reason and advance the absolute calendar without catch-up. An omitted observation, mistimed/duplicate/foreign-venue event, book bound to a different instrument, quote violating its configured tick, or displayed order admitted at or after `s` in a purported `s⁻` capture is instead **INVALID_CONTRACT_OR_EVIDENCE**: these are evidence/source/admission/phase contradictions, not ordinary missing liquidity. A book that becomes one-sided **only at `t`** does not annul a complete earlier window.

The runner must make **one canonical source observation at every configured one-second boundary**, including an explicit unavailable observation when a live book lacks a qualifying pair. Such an observation makes a dependent scheduled window `RATE_UNAVAILABLE`. A boundary with **no source observation/event at all** is instead `INVALID_CONTRACT_OR_EVIDENCE`: an omitted capture or incomplete stream cannot masquerade as market illiquidity. The `s⁻` premise requires the runner-owned clock, deterministic phases, intermediate-event drain and phase-owned event producers; a venue lock alone is insufficient. Scheduler callbacks stamped at `s` may queue work but must not mutate book state before capture. A source observation at `t` belongs to later windows; if captured before or after evaluation of the already-complete prior window, it must still precede all work stamped `t` and cannot alter `t−60 s, …, t−1 s`. Preserve the observation event's actual global frame-header sequence; a per-venue sequence or a guessed event count is not that identity.

Let `M_s` be the already integer-quantized positive perp midpoint (quote price atoms per whole base unit), and `I_s` the corresponding spot midpoint. Let `N=60`. The rate remains the draft's separately scaled, capped and half-even-quantized `r_v(t)` in micro-bp. The funding notional price is

`P_v(t) = round_half_even((Σ_s M_s)/N)` quote price atoms per whole base unit.

The final mark quantization is explicit: it changes the exact unrounded mean by at most half one quote-price atom, and all funding cash/remainder identities are relative to this **quantized** `P_v(t)`. The rate's exact rational sample calculation is not replaced by `P_v(t)/mean(I_s)`. No fallback to the legacy whole-bp `FundingRate.Rate` is legal. Positive `r_v(t)` debits a long and credits a short.

The eligible signed size is the complete venue-local client position at `t⁻`: all events strictly before `t` are committed, but no order, price automation, liquidation, new risk decision or actor callback stamped `t` may change it first. Hedge-side long and short sizes for the same account/symbol are summed before one cash preview. A position entered after the final `t−1 s` **pre-instant** price sample but before `t` is still eligible: record its incidence, size and payment separately rather than implying every paid position was held throughout the sampling minute. The simultaneous-epoch rule below governs coincident N/S attempts. Publish payment/rate evidence only after both venues have completed their independent cash decisions. E2's positive configured publication/feed delays prevent an actor from acting on this new publication at `t`.

After both venues' funding decisions, run a coherent **read-only risk-valuation preflight** using the post-payment balances and current book state, before any same-time order ingress. The funding-window `P_v(t)` cannot replace a current risk mark. If that preflight cannot price **any account with relevant open exposure**, retain already-posted payment evidence but terminate the world at `t` with `RISK_VALUATION_UNAVAILABLE`: subsequent market/economic outcomes are censored, not zero. If no account has relevant exposure, an unrelated unmarked book is not a risk failure. If all exposed accounts are priceable and no terminal reason exists, perform the ordinary risk/liquidation sweep before same-time order ingress. Do not leave possibly under-maintained exposure trading while waiting for a quote to reappear, or silently liquidate at a stale mark. The same terminal rule applies to an unpriceable exposed account on the normal E2 risk clock. This is deliberately a strict research boundary, not an assertion that a live exchange would stop trading globally. The present automation order in `exchange/exchange.go` does **not** satisfy these funding/risk phases and must be changed only by an opt-in E2 adapter with equal-time regressions.

### Simultaneous N/S epoch, including a terminal boundary

At an instant scheduled on both absolute calendars (first at minute 25), freeze **both** venue-local windows, complete clearing positions and account balances from the same `t⁻` frontier. Preflight both venue batches before changing either. Any structural/identity/arithmetic defect in either venue invalidates the epoch and commits **neither** venue; this is not an economic result. Otherwise, decide each venue independently: a missing window posts no payment and advances only that venue's fixed calendar; a payer cash shortfall posts no payment for that venue and marks an economic terminal reason; an eligible solvent batch posts all its cash and remainders atomically. Therefore a valid S payment **does post** even if N has a cash shortfall, and vice versa. Commit valid batches in stable venue-ID order only after the joint preflight, then perform the read-only risk preflight for all exposed accounts using post-payment balances. If that preflight finds an unavailable mark, record its terminal reason as well. No actor or liquidation work stamped `t` interleaves with these steps. If any economic terminal reason exists, stop the world after all otherwise-valid scheduled obligations and their evidence are committed, **before** liquidation or actor ingress at `t`. This is an explicitly selected simultaneous-payment convention, not a claim that independent venues would coordinate in reality.

Record a sorted `(venue, reason, client-if-applicable)` **set** of simultaneous terminal reasons, so N shortfall plus S risk-unavailability does not depend on venue iteration order. The top-level status is `INVALID_CONTRACT_OR_EVIDENCE` if preflight found a structural defect (with zero payment commits), otherwise `E2_EARLY_STOP` if one or more economic/valuation terminal reasons exist. Preserve the specific reason set; do not arbitrarily call one venue's failure the sole cause. If there is no terminal reason, execute the ordinary risk pass and continue the world. The independent result invariant is that permuting N/S calculation and preflight order changes neither venue's payment decision nor the final reason set.

## Whole-venue funding obligation and finite resources

For every registered client `i` at one venue/symbol, let `q_i` be net signed base atoms, `D = base_precision × 10,000 × rate_units_per_bp`, and `R_i` the persistent signed fractional numerator from [`ScaledFundingAccrual`](../../../../exchange/funding_scaled.go). At a successful attempt, independently replay

`X_i = −q_i × P_v(t) × r_v(t)` and `X_i + R_i(before) = D × C_i + R_i(after)`,

where `C_i` is the posted signed quote-atom change, division truncates toward zero, and `|R_i(after)| < D`. State is keyed by `(venue, client, perpetual symbol)` and persists through flat periods and reversals. Every open position must map to a real client; the legacy silent skip of a missing client is an integrity failure. The sum of **all** signed client positions at the venue must be zero at `t⁻`. E2 has no implicit house position. A nonzero sum is a position/clearing integrity failure, not permission to have the venue pay an unbounded unmatched leg.

Post `C_v = −Σ_i C_i` to a separately identified, **finite, prefunded venue rounding reserve**, not to fee revenue as if it were earned trading income. Record its initial external endowment and all movements; give **identical** reserve endowments to OFF and ON worlds. The fixed `K` account cap includes flat accounts retaining fractional remainders. Registration beyond `K`, recycling an identity, or discarding a remainder is prohibited in this first E2 candidate. With zero-sum positions, common `P_v(t), r_v(t), D`, zero initial remainders and at most `K` persistent client states, exact arithmetic gives `D × (reserve_change_since_initial) = Σ_i R_i`. Because each `|R_i| < D`, the cumulative reserve change is an integer strictly within `(-K,K)` quote atoms. An initial reserve of `K` quote atoms is therefore sufficient for *rounding alone* over arbitrarily many payments **if** all those preconditions hold and the reserve is not spent elsewhere. This is a proof obligation and a tiny explicit finite venue endowment, not a substitute for a missing counterparty or a general guarantee against default. Retained fractional claims and venue reserve movements must be visible at the horizon; do not call the reserve a strategy profit.

The batch must preflight every client's resulting total USD perp cash balance, every remainder/scale, exact net positions, and the venue reserve **before changing anything**. A payer may use cash already earmarked as margin; a resulting maintenance deficit triggers the immediate risk pass. But if a payer's *total* perp cash would become negative, the first E2 Regime-A model has no declared workout or automatic transfer from its spot wallet: emit `FUNDING_PAYER_CASH_SHORTFALL`, stop the economic trajectory at that boundary, and report it as a **venue-local payment default/liquidity failure**, not necessarily global bankruptcy. Subsequent terms are censored. Do **not** skip that account, pay the receiver with invented cash, cancel only this payment and continue, or call it an evidence defect. A negative venue reserve despite the zero-sum proof is instead a contract/implementation defect. Arithmetic overflow, missing client, bad scale or inconsistent positions are also defects. For all such failures, no client cash, reserve, remainder or settlement-success event is committed. This stop-on-unmodeled-default rule is deliberately narrower than a full clearinghouse/insurance model; a later default-resolution experiment needs its own prospective contract.

## Distinct outcomes and acceptance tests

| Scheduled attempt | Cash/remainders | Calendar | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complete valid window, solvent atomic batch | All posted once | Advance fixed grid | Actual funding payment; later actor receipt is separate |
| Explicit canonical unavailable-book observation in prior window | No change | Advance fixed grid, no catch-up | `RATE_UNAVAILABLE`; liquidity/benchmark-coverage outcome, not zero cash rate |
| Omitted, duplicate, mistimed or foreign-venue source observation | No venue in joint epoch commits | Stop before posting | `INVALID_CONTRACT_OR_EVIDENCE`; missing/corrupt evidence is not an illiquid book |
| Payer cannot cover total cash debit | No change at that venue; any valid other-venue payment may still post | Stop after the joint epoch | `FUNDING_PAYER_CASH_SHORTFALL`; venue-local economic failure/censoring, not completed term |
| Cash posted, then current exposed-account risk mark unavailable | Successful cash/remainder posting remains | Stop after the joint epoch | `RISK_VALUATION_UNAVAILABLE` in the terminal reason set; valid payment, censored later outcomes |
| Position, client, arithmetic, reserve or evidence invariant fails | No venue in the joint epoch commits | Stop before joint-epoch posting | `INVALID_CONTRACT_OR_EVIDENCE`; repair before economic interpretation |

Before any E2 economic world, test the producer and independent replay with: absent/late/foreign/cached samples; one-sided at `t−1` versus only at `t`; thin/aged quotes recorded without inventing depth; half-even midpoint-mean ties; an eligible position opened after the last sample; order and liquidation exactly at `t`; N/S coincidence and phase; opposite and hedge-mode positions; closing/reopening across terms; flat retained remainders and the fixed account cap; equal OFF/ON reserve endowment; fee-revenue versus rounding-reserve separation; a payer margin deficit versus a payer total-cash shortfall; successful funding followed by missing current risk mark; **coincident** N shortfall/S valid payment, reverse assignment, both shortfalls, one missing window/other valid payment, one structural defect/other otherwise valid payment, and multiple terminal reasons with permutation-invariant result; insufficient finite reserve; mutation/corruption of one payment, sample, position and remainder; complete per-venue cash conservation; no same-timestamp actor use of unpublished funding. A scheduled count is never a successful-payment count. The registered world may still have no complete terms or strict terminal mark; neither result authorizes a price fallback or a longer run.

For every **assigned** OFF and ON world report scheduled attempts, complete input windows, posted payments, first cash shortfall, first exposed-account risk-unavailability time, completed-term incidence, and final process/evidence status. A shortfall or risk stop is treatment-dependent and must not disappear through a survivor-only filter. The future protocol must predefine its fixed horizon and what constitutes a complete OFF/ON pair. If either arm stops before that horizon, the fixed-horizon paired basis/market-quality contrast is **NOT_IDENTIFIED for that pair**, not zero or a value extrapolated from the surviving arm. A common pre-stop window may be described separately, with both stop times and its selection caveat, but it does not replace the fixed-horizon causal endpoint. The per-completed-term cashflow remains a conditional object with its denominator and non-completion reasons visible; it is not a population-average payoff or a carry-versus-idle profit difference.

This is a prospective E2 **semantic choice**, not a mechanical no-op or a restatement of exchange rules. It requires independent review, implementation/evidence fixtures and a separately committed economic protocol before promotion. No E2 world or holdout has run under this document.
