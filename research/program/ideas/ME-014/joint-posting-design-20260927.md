The [bounded design review](../../reviews/me014-joint-posting-design-20260927.md) accepted the final choices for an isolated fixture, not any implementation or economic result. The [settlement contract](settlement-contract-20260927.md) remains authoritative; this note specifies the smallest safe posting component after the reviewed source-window checkpoint.

## Money and ownership

For each venue/perpetual, freeze exactly `K` registered client identities at construction, including flat accounts; later registration and identity recycling are forbidden in this first E2 candidate. Keep one persistent signed remainder per identity and a separate USD rounding-reserve balance. The reserve is externally endowed once with **exactly `K` USD quote atoms**, before any payment, from the named external primitive `E2_ROUNDING_RESERVE_ENDOWMENT`. Emit a canonical `0 → K` movement, include it in terminal venue holdings and the independent source/sink ledger, and use the same `K` and endowment in OFF and ON. No top-up, withdrawal or other spending is allowed. The reserve cannot fund unmatched positions, failed payers, fees, or other activities. It is not trading profit or fee revenue. A fixture with two registered accounts, a declared cap of three, and only two atoms of reserve must fail before posting: the bound follows the frozen cap, not a later count of active positions.

For a valid payment, each client starts with total venue-local perp cash `B_i` and owned fraction `R_i`. The preview computes `C_i` and `R_i'` from the complete signed position `q_i`, common notional mark `P`, rate `r` and denominator `D`; it refuses any `B_i+C_i<0`. With `Σq_i=0`, post `−ΣC_i` to the reserve. Invariant: `ΣC_i + Δreserve = 0` and, from zero initial fractions, `D × (reserve−initial_reserve) = ΣR_i`. Flat accounts stay in both sums. These are exact integer identities, not a claim about constant marked wealth.

The third venue bucket is `funding_rounding_reserve`, not legacy `funding_remainder` fee revenue. Its symbol/asset owner and initial endowment must be explicit. The exchange conservation check, terminal venue ledger, and independent analysis must all include it; old configurations with the option absent must retain their old JSON/economic behavior. A malformed or unknown bucket fails closed in the E2 analyzer. The independent analyzer recomputes client cash/remainders and reserve from source positions, terms and prior states rather than accepting the simulator's preview as the oracle.

## Joint epoch transaction

At a coincident N/S boundary, obtain both source windows and complete account snapshots from the same pre-ingress frontier. Under stable venue-ID lock order, prepare **both** venues before mutating either. A structural defect in either venue aborts all commits; a cash shortfall is an economic decision for only that venue, so the other venue's valid payment still posts. Explicit unavailable source is a no-payment decision, not a malformed snapshot. The coordinator records the sorted decision/reason set and advances absolute calendars separately. A prepared plan must be bound to exact venue, symbol, roster, cash, positions, remainders, reserve, terms, timestamp and source sequence; stale or modified state cannot be committed. No actor/automation/order/risk phase may interleave between final validation and all valid commits.

The opt-in transaction uses a required, error-reporting canonical journal. One joint epoch record carries both decisions and, for each posted venue, every account's `q`, cash before/delta/after, remainder before/after, plus reserve before/delta/after, rate/mark and source references. **Fixture order:** after complete joint validation, mutate every eligible venue's cash, owned fractions and reserve while both venues are held stable; then append the single joint epoch record. If append fails before, during or after accepting bytes, or final stream sealing fails, freeze the fixture immediately, forbid further epochs, and expose the mutated state only as an **invalid trajectory**, never as a successful payment result. A completed journal append alone is not a sealed stream. Replay recognizes successful payments only from a complete, sealed canonical stream and independently reconciled terminal state. This is a fail-closed simulation/evidence rule, **not crash-atomic storage or rollback**. The ordinary balance logger has no error return and cannot be the fixture's authoritative payment evidence. The later live adapter must preserve the same failure classification; complete independent replay must reject omitted, duplicated, reordered or corrupted account/reserve records and reconcile them with terminal wallet and venue-bucket balances.

## Finite fixture before production wiring

The first implementation milestone is an isolated joint posting fixture, not a new `NewSim` funding mode. Test: (1) exact `K`-atom endowment without payment, including rejection of a two-account/three-cap underendowment; (2) one-atom reserve transfer without fee-revenue change; (3) repeated payment with flat retained fractions and no identity recycling; (4) stale cash/position/remainder/reserve between prepare and commit; (5) N valid/S structural defect and reverse, with zero commits; (6) N shortfall/S valid and reverse; (7) required journal failures before/during/after append and at final seal, all producing frozen invalid trajectories and no false success; (8) independent replay rejecting an omitted account or reserve movement. Use a single known-rate/mark synthetic input only as a mechanical fixture. It does not establish natural two-sided windows, actual funding incidence, carry profitability, market survival or E2 causal inference.

Only after this fixture is reviewed should a separate production adapter disable legacy funding for the E2 option, bind the owned ledger to the source window and 5/9-minute calendars, publish delayed rate/payment information, run post-payment coherent risk, and lock a finite economic protocol. The current E0/E1 strict terminal-priceability limitation remains an independent gate.
