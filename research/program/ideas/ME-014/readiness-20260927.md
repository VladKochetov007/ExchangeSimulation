## Economic contract being prepared

The first E2 comparison is a *venue-local, finite-account, repeated* spot/perpetual carry experiment, not a risk-free arbitrage identity. Carry can pay or lose after actual legs, fees, missed payments, residual exposure and exit. A funding-rate expectation is not a cash transfer. OFF accounts remain endowed but idle; ON permission does not change background deposits or supply a common inter-venue wallet. N and S are two components of one world, not independent replications.

The draft's compressed schedule is retained as a prospective mechanical target: N interval 300 s with phase 0; S interval 480 s with phase 60 s; first scheduled instants at 5 and 9 minutes after the calendar epoch. Subsequent instants stay on their own absolute grids even after an unavailable payment. In the exclusive-start/inclusive-end `(20,140]` minute observation window, there are **24 N and 15 S scheduled instants**. These are not successful payments. At each scheduled instant, the proposed rate uses exactly the 60 venue-local, positive, two-sided spot/perp midpoint pairs at `t−60 s, …, t−1 s`. Resting quotes may be older than a sample timestamp; they remain the current executable book while still live. A missing side, cached midpoint after a side disappears, non-positive price, mistimed sample or other-venue value makes the scheduled rate unavailable; no midpoint is invented. A complete sample set is necessary for this draft contract but may be rare in the current intermittently one-sided population. Natural coverage has **not** yet been measured for an E2 population, so zero realized funding is a valid possible design failure, not permission to fill gaps retrospectively.

The proposed economic rate is `clip((1 bp + mean premium_bps) × Δ/480 min, ±75 bp × Δ/480 min)`, with `premium_bps = 10000 × (perp_mid/spot_mid − 1)`. All price ratios require positive prices. The 1-bp base and 75-bp cap are inherited *model assumptions* from the existing eight-hour convention, not empirical market estimates. `Δ` is 5 or 8 minutes. The implementation must keep exact rational sample and time arithmetic until one final half-even quantization to **one-millionth of a basis point**; the zero-premium scheduled rates are 10,417 micro-bp for N and 16,667 micro-bp for S. The returned value is a `QuantizedFundingRate` carrying both signed units and units per bp; conversion to the legacy whole-bp field is permitted only when exact. Thus lowering the old integer-bp `Interval` alone is economically wrong. Time compression also changes the premium averaging horizon and policy holding opportunity; it is not a literal time-rescaled real exchange.

At zero premium, six N payments contribute only 0.0625 bp over a proposed term and four S payments about 0.0667 bp, before rate quantization. Four 1-bp taker fills cost 4 bp before spreads and leg risk. A rational finite carry desk may therefore abstain unless a sufficiently large expected premium or other declared benefit outweighs costs. The protocol must count delivered opportunities and actual payment coverage, not require an entry to make the mechanism look active. Missing windows may covary with the same illiquidity that changes carry returns; simply conditioning on completed terms can select a nonrepresentative state.

The fixture tests show the specified 24/15 schedule, fixed phase after a missed attempt, micro-bp rate/caps, complete past-window requirement, positive-price domain, scale-safe result type and signed half-even quantization. ## Next bounded acceptance gates

1. **Source/semantic review of this contract** before changing the exchange. Reject if an absolute skipped-payment calendar creates an unacceptable incentive or if a 60/60 two-sided requirement makes the intended multi-payment experiment structurally unidentifiable; any alternative must be a prospective economic amendment, not a post-outcome rescue.
2. **Payment precision and atomicity fixture:** the pure, reviewed `f0df3d3e` preview now covers one long, one short, one unmatched position, sub-unit repeated accrual, rate sign reversal, position close/reopen, netted hedge example, invalid snapshot and overflow. **Still required:** a true batch that checks every wallet, the finite venue residual and all remainder updates before mutating any state; independent cash/remainder reconstruction from canonical events; failure/corruption fixtures. The exact denominator is `base_precision × 10000 × rate_units_per_bp` before any truncation of notional. The public mark that may legally enter a scheduled transfer remains a separate prospective choice.
3. **Production schedule/window fixture and prospective mark choice:** choose explicitly whether settlement notional uses a valid `t^-` venue-local mark or a declared statistic of the completed past window. The draft's rate window does not itself settle this question. A current-mark rule may skip payments if liquidity disappears at the boundary, potentially creating an incentive to withdraw; a lagged-window rule changes exposure pricing and must not be smuggled in as a fallback. Review the choice before wiring cash posting. Then create legal spot/perp books with timestamped two-sided observations and prove 5/9-minute first attempts, 24/15 scheduled window counts, same-timestamp `t^-` position eligibility, no future sample, publication after posting, delayed actor receipt, missing-window advancement and no catch-up payment. Failed or deferred events must have different identities from successful cash transfers.
4. **Integrated readiness:** finite segregated accounts, real entry/management/exit across multiple *actual* payments on both venues, OFF/ON equality except policy permission, malformed-evidence rejection, clean full/race/determinism gates and independent reviews. Only then may a fixed ME-014 economic protocol and resource preflight be proposed. No unused E2 ceiling is itself permission to run an unregistered cell.

The immediate implementation is intentionally small because the currently validated E0/E1 world does not supply strict terminal valuation. Preserving a mechanically correct funding ledger and recording missingness are prerequisites to interpreting any later carry outcome; adding broad liquidity or a price anchor merely to obtain payments would be a separate, reviewed economic design.

## Fractional cash lifetime for the separate payment preview

The second opt-in arithmetic increment is a pure [`ScaledFundingAccrual`](../../../../exchange/funding_scaled.go) at source commit `f0df3d3e6819658f6685aadc86dd060d4afa2bec`, **not production cash posting**. It previews the client quote-atom transfer using the exact signed numerator `−position_size × mark_price × rate_units + prior_remainder` over `base_precision × 10000 × rate_units_per_bp`, truncates the posted atom amount toward zero, and carries the signed remainder. Mark price must be positive and use quote atoms per base unit. A positive rate debits a long and credits a short. Invalid scale, mark, restored remainder or overflow fail before mutation. Its [independent review](../../reviews/me014-payment-primitive-20260927.md) preserves an initial economic REJECT about remainder lifetime and the scoped acceptance after correction.

The prospective lifetime rule is **one persistent remainder per venue, client and perpetual symbol**, across flat periods and signed-position reversals; hedge-mode long and short positions of the same account/symbol must be summed into one signed exposure before preview. The venue is part of the key: N and S must never pool remainders. Persisted state includes its denominator, and restoring a numerator under changed precision or rate scale is forbidden. Resetting a remainder at each term close would let repeated small positions evade a contractual cost. A later cash posting can nevertheless discharge an earlier term's fractional liability. For a term that owns **all** exposure of that venue/account/symbol during its interval, report both actual posted cash and exact economic accrual: `term_accrual = sum(posted_cash) + (remainder_after − remainder_before)/denominator`. E2 proposes at most one active carry term per carry account; overlapping terms or other concurrent exposures require a prospectively specified allocation, not this unqualified formula. The remaining fraction at the observation horizon is an outstanding fractional claim/liability, not a full cash atom and not zero economic cost. It must be visible in the account/term evidence and terminal interpretation. This rule is selected **before any E2 outcome**, but is not wired into the exchange or a locked protocol yet.

The pure function does not prove that a venue can pay an unmatched short. The existing revenue sink's overflow check is not an economic funding-capital limit. Production integration must verify that all matched customer/house positions are accounted for and net as declared; otherwise it needs an explicit finite venue counterparty and funding source. It also needs a whole-batch wallet/venue preflight, atomic commit, and evidence of both posted cash and carried fractions. Neither a negative venue balance nor an algebraic `−sum(client deltas)` alone proves adequate funds. The settlement mark/source choice in gate 3 above remains open.
