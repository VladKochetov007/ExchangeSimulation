## Question and mechanism

In a finite ABC/USD book with recurrent takers, round-trip demand and two symmetric pure makers, does an AS-style maker that shifts its quotes toward the **imbalance in its own delayed public best-level snapshot** change later public two-sided liquidity compared with an otherwise identical AS-style maker that observes the same data but assigns the signal zero weight? An imbalance lean is a policy action, not evidence that imbalance predicts a price move. Displayed depth includes the maker's own order, so self-generated feedback and queue priority compete with genuinely informative order flow as explanations.

At a decision with `B,A>0`, `I_ppm=trunc(10^6(B−A)/(B+A))` and `shift=trunc_to_tick(trunc(reference_mid × I_ppm × gain_bps / 10^10))`. Add the same signed shift to both already-rounded AS bid and ask. This preserves the AS half-spread and inventory skew and uses the shared post-only cancel/response lifecycle. The input is unavailable as a directional signal if the latest local snapshot is one-sided, its source age reaches 10 simulated seconds, or arithmetic/quote-domain checks fail. The 15-second last-delivered two-sided *price reference* is a separate belief available equally to pure and AS-family makers; it is never a venue mark or terminal valuation substitute. Order-identity reconstruction must report the focal maker's own displayed quantity at **each best side of each source-time snapshot actually delivered**, its share of that side, and whether that best level consists only of its own orders. This diagnoses endogenous self-feedback; it does not purify the signal or establish alpha.

Control and treatment use the **same v1 signal policy, v5 evidence, delivered-depth observation, resources, clock, and quote lifecycle**. Only `signal_gain_bps` differs: `0` versus `2`. At $50,000/ABC and imbalance 0.5, 2 bps shifts the quote center about $5, five $1 ticks, half the initial approximately $10 AS half-spread. At near-unit imbalance it approaches $10. These values derive from the existing tick/spread scale and source-time cadence, not ME-016 outcomes; they are not claimed optimal or calibrated for profit. A zero effective shift after parts-per-million and tick truncation remains a valid zero action. Do not increase gain after observing a small effect.

## Identification, invalidity and stop rules

This is a programmed mechanical implication. The *market* hypothesis is genuinely uncertain: displacement may improve, worsen or leave unchanged two-sided public availability, because queue position, own-depth feedback, adverse selection and resource constraints all matter. Falsify a clean positive-availability claim with nonpositive paired differences; do not retune to avoid it. If qualifying signal opportunities are absent, classify mechanism activation as absent/limited rather than a demonstrated economic null about an exercised signal. If the signal shifts quotes but Q is unchanged, retain the valid null for Q with its activation denominator.

The development unit of uncertainty is a whole seed world, not thousands of correlated fills or seconds. Three seeds give a descriptive paired range, not high-powered significance, tail risk or confirmation. No after-outcome p-value threshold is introduced.
