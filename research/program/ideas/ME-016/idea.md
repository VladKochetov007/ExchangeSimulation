## Policy, actor and deployment

- Policy: an AS-style inventory/variance quote with an additional configurable additive center shift. For displayed top-level sizes `B,A > 0`, `I=(B−A)/(B+A)`; `signal_shift = reference_mid × signal_gain_bps × I / 10,000`. The implementation first truncates `I` toward zero to parts per million, then truncates the price shift toward zero to whole ticks and adds it to both already-rounded AS quote sides. The base half-spread and inventory term are unchanged. The signal is zero if the latest local book is not two-sided or its source age reaches the separately configured `max_signal_age`, even if a finite ME-015-style reference still supplies a price belief. Zero gain or equal displayed sizes must produce exactly the base AS quote. This is a programmed quote rule, **not** a proof of the AS model or of predictive imbalance.
- Actor: the existing `RecurringMaker` holds finite cash/base, ±working-inventory limit inclusive of live/pending/cancel-in-flight orders, and may abstain or withdraw. It uses the same post-only cancel/replace lifecycle. No recapitalization, obligation to quote, or direct venue-book access is added.
- Deployment and venue: the existing delayed local market-data/order/response gateways and one FIFO ABC/USD spot venue. The displayed public level includes the maker's own resting quantity because the feed does not expose a verified self-excluded book. This can create self-feedback; it is a confound to measure, not a hidden alpha assumption. No actor knows the instantaneous exchange state.

Top-of-book imbalance is one possible local information feature, not an externally imposed price path. Price changes, predictive value and profitable execution remain hypotheses to be tested through prospective contrasts. If both bid/ask displayed sizes are absent, the rule cannot claim a qualifying imbalance observation. A no-trade/withdrawal decision may be rational under risk and post-only constraints.
