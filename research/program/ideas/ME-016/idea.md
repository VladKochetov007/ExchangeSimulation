# ME-016 — delivered-depth signal in repeated maker competition

Stage: **PREPARE; NO ECONOMIC WORLD OR LOCKED PROTOCOL**. This is the first bounded E1 implementation increment under the [2026-09-27 owner authorization](../../longrun-baselines/authorization-20260927.md), not a third E0 rescue run. It follows [ME-015](../ME-015/report.md), whose public two-sided-time response was observed but whose maker-gain comparison remains `NOT_IDENTIFIED`. ME-013 and ME-015 source, protocols and evidence remain immutable.

Prospective question: with the same recurring finite demand, maker resources, delayed deployment and common quote lifecycle, does a maker's use of *delivered top-of-book displayed imbalance* change its own quote placement and subsequent inventory/liquidity, and does that change the outcomes of otherwise identical competing makers? The first implementation claim is only **MECHANICAL**: the configured signal uses no future state, zero signal nests the existing AS-style quote, and all maker limits/order admission still apply. No positive return, price prediction, equilibrium or empirical-realism claim is assumed.

## Policy, actor and deployment

- Policy: an AS-style inventory/variance quote with an additional configurable additive center shift. For displayed top-level sizes `B,A > 0`, `I=(B−A)/(B+A)`; `signal_shift = reference_mid × signal_gain_bps × I / 10,000`. The implementation first truncates `I` toward zero to parts per million, then truncates the price shift toward zero to whole ticks and adds it to both already-rounded AS quote sides. The base half-spread and inventory term are unchanged. The signal is zero if the latest local book is not two-sided or its source age reaches the separately configured `max_signal_age`, even if a finite ME-015-style reference still supplies a price belief. Zero gain or equal displayed sizes must produce exactly the base AS quote. This is a programmed quote rule, **not** a proof of the AS model or of predictive imbalance.
- Actor: the existing `RecurringMaker` holds finite cash/base, ±working-inventory limit inclusive of live/pending/cancel-in-flight orders, and may abstain or withdraw. It uses the same post-only cancel/replace lifecycle. No recapitalization, obligation to quote, or direct venue-book access is added.
- Deployment and venue: the existing delayed local market-data/order/response gateways and one FIFO ABC/USD spot venue. The displayed public level includes the maker's own resting quantity because the feed does not expose a verified self-excluded book. This can create self-feedback; it is a confound to measure, not a hidden alpha assumption. No actor knows the instantaneous exchange state.

Top-of-book imbalance is one possible local information feature, not an externally imposed price path. Price changes, predictive value and profitable execution remain hypotheses to be tested through prospective contrasts. If both bid/ask displayed sizes are absent, the rule cannot claim a qualifying imbalance observation. A no-trade/withdrawal decision may be rational under risk and post-only constraints.

## Bounded implementation and acceptance contract

The opt-in policy and signal evidence are now implemented in `simulations/repeatedspot/maker_imbalance.go`, the shared actor, and `experiment/repeatedspot`. The new policy name is `bounded_imbalance_stoikov_maker_v1`; its evidence uses the separate `repeated-spot-opaque-v5` epoch. Historical fixed/AS v2/v3 capture stays on v4. The v5 replay independently joins displayed size to the published public book and then to the actor's delivered snapshot and decision, reconstructs the quote from the contract, and rejects missing, forged, future or rehashed inconsistent signal evidence. This is a **mechanical readiness increment**, not a new economic result or a locked ME-016 protocol.

Focused fixtures cover actual delayed-gateway delivery, post-only admission, finite pending-inclusive inventory, positive/negative/equal-size signal, strict source-age expiry, one-sided cached-price use without a directional signal, tick truncation, unquoteable/overflow results, parameter corruption, fresh-process identity and cross-revision v2/v3 evidence parity. The [bounded review](../../reviews/me016-mechanical-20260927.md) accepted this implementation/evidence scope after an initial rejection for missing coverage. The final clean full gate remains a separate publication check. No generic simulator redesign or change to the historical ME-015 trajectory was made.

**No ME-016 economic world is run-ready yet.** A prospective finite E1 protocol, a real outcome denominator and missing-mark rule, an independent causal-design review, a pinned clean candidate, capacity check and immutable output namespace are still required. The E0 terminal-price limitation is inherited as a design constraint, not repaired by adding this signal.

A later E1 economic protocol must prospectively choose signal gain, background composition, primary payoff or liquidity endpoint, missing-mark handling, seed rule, finite matrix and comparison with same-resource AS. It must account for own-order inclusion and the E0 terminal-price problem rather than treating intermittent two-sided time as maker profit. A mixed-book interaction question is more informative than isolated-policy charting, but it is not authorized to execute by this card.

Competing explanations for a future observed difference include: actual directional information in exogenous flow; self-generated depth feedback; tick/post-only admission changes rather than prediction; altered inventory exposure; clock phase; and unavailable terminal valuation. A zero or reverse effect is valid. ME-016 does not reopen the completed ME-001/002/003/005 or R2/SV1D lines, and does not consume any holdout.
