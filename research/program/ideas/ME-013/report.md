# ME-013 r1 — repeated spot-maker competition: valid early book extinction

Status: **DEVELOPMENT RESULT; PRIMARY ECONOMIC CONTRAST NOT IDENTIFIED.** This is not a confirmation, empirical-market validation, or endorsement of an E1/E2 integrated ecology. The registered r1 world is preserved unchanged; no result-dependent repair has been inserted into its 24 cells.

## Question and registered estimand

The [prospective protocol](protocol.md) asked whether, with the same finite recurring demand, resources, delays and maker lifecycle, replacing four symmetric pure makers (P) with four inventory-aware AS-style makers (A) changes fee-net passive-endowment gain at a 0.1-ABC quote size. It also registered a 0.2-ABC working-size contrast and two mixed slot rosters (M1/M2) as secondary screens. For maker `i`, `G_i = Δcash_i + ΔABC_i × P_T`, where `P_T` is the terminal public two-sided midpoint and cash already includes actual fees. The registered primary observation was `(Σ_i G_i,A − Σ_i G_i,P)/4` for each of three development seeds, not an end-of-run cash difference or last-trade mark. Missing strict terminal marks make that pair undefined. The protocol declares the *entire* primary three-seed contrast `NOT_IDENTIFIED` when any primary pair is unavailable.

That rule is decisive here: **24/24 economic cells completed and passed strict replay, but all 24 terminal books lacked a two-sided mark.** The primary contrast has zero available pairs and no estimate. The two registered fresh-process controls reproduce the first cell's manifest and analyzed JSON byte-for-byte at Go worker counts 1 and 4. They are technical controls, not two extra economic observations. No E1/E2 economic world, confirmation world or protected holdout was run.

## Economic environment and mechanism boundary

The implemented world is one FIFO ABC/USD spot book, 55 simulated minutes, with a fixed `[0,10 min)` warm-up and `[10,55 min)` measurement window. Four makers each have 50 ABC, $2.5 million and a ±10-ABC pending-inclusive working limit; they use the same five-second post-only cancel/wait/replace lifecycle and delayed local market data. P quotes a fixed-distance half-spread; A applies the registered normalized-inventory and rolling-variance rule. At the initial state their approximate half-spreads are matched, but the later package behaviors need not be identical. The seed-once participant posts just one finite 0.1-ABC order per side and never replenishes. Four finite random takers evaluate every two seconds; two finite round-trip actors send marketable IOC orders only when a usable two-sided local book exists. A random taker can continue to submit a market order even at zero delivered facing depth; exchange acceptance is not a fill.

The book reference price is used for initial finite seed orders, not an ongoing fair-value feed. The recurring maker's `targetQuote` requires a positive local bid and an ask above it; after the book becomes one-sided or empty, it cannot form a quote and does not invoke its pricing rule. That is an explicit policy/information dependency, not proof that every rational maker should behave this way. The registered demand frequency and order sizes are model primitives, not inferred end-user flow. For orientation, four takers each drawing about 0.1 ABC every two seconds imply roughly 0.5 ABC of attempted quantity *per side* over five seconds under symmetric direction draws, before depth clipping, rejections and endogenous responses. Four 0.1-ABC makers initially offer at most 0.4 ABC per side; this rough comparison helps explain why depletion is plausible but is **not** a measured causal decomposition of each trade.

This ordering supports the bounded explanation that loss of a usable local two-sided reference prevents the registered maker policy from rebuilding the book after finite opening depth and early quotes are depleted. It does not identify the unique marginal cause of every depletion event, and it does not imply that the actor saw an instantaneous global book. ## Scientific interpretation, limitations and next decision

This r1 result establishes a **valid market-survival and identification boundary for this exact E0 roster**. The implementation produced accepted orders, trades, fees and complete evidence, but the economic state required for terminal valuation and prolonged maker competition disappeared. An empty book is not automatically a software defect. Here it makes the preregistered economic estimand undefined and defeats the intended repeated-strategy comparison. It does not establish that finite makers, inventory-aware quoting, endogenous prices, or a broader market ecology are impossible.

No seed, quote size, fee, spread, capital amount, horizon, mark definition or liquidity backstop was adjusted after observing these outcomes. E1/E2 economic claims cannot be inherited from this failed E0 ecology. The next *separately named* development question should be whether a finite maker can use a **short-lived, actor-local last two-sided reference** during transient one-sidedness and still withdraw when that reference is stale or risk/capital binds. The reference is a declared maker belief formed from delayed public data, not an external fair-value oracle or a guaranteed two-sided obligation. Applying the same recovery rule to P and A would preserve the policy comparison while testing whether the r1 absorbing-state dependency can be broken. Finite private-value limit-order participants are a defensible alternative ecology but introduce a distinct external-valuation primitive; do not add both mechanisms in one arm. Before any successor world: specify its estimand and anti-backstop criteria; test one-sided/re-entry, stale-reference and resource edge cases; independently review the new source/evidence contract; preregister a finite development matrix. E1/E2 implementation may reuse mechanically accepted components, but integrated economic worlds should wait for a repeated spot foundation that actually exercises its measurement window.

Later capacity, games and empirical comparisons remain outside this result. Host resource capacity is not strategy capacity; marked portfolio wealth is not a conserved ledger; market trade count is not an independent sample count; same-seed cells are not guaranteed identical complete background paths after endogenous divergence. Development seeds are not confirmation or real-data validation. The negative r1 outcome and all older ME-001/002/003/005 and R2/SV1D records remain attached to their original source/config/analysis identities.
