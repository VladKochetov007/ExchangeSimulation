## Economic question and estimand

In a single repeated ABC/USD spot book with the same finite demand, resources, information delay and maker lifecycle, how does replacing four symmetric fixed-distance makers by four AS-style inventory-aware makers change fee-net passive-endowment gain? Does doubling each maker's displayed quote quantity alter that comparison, and what happens when both policies compete in the same book? This is a **simulation-internal development causal contrast of complete quote-policy packages**, not a test of AS optimality, capital capacity, equilibrium or empirical market realism. The programmed inventory skew is implementation; any resulting change in fills, risk, PnL or market depth is the economic object.

For maker `i`, with cash `C`, base `B` and terminal public two-sided midpoint `P_T` in USD/ABC, define `G_i=[C_i(T)-C_i(0)]+[B_i(T)-B_i(0)]P_T`, converted in the analyzer to quote atoms with the declared base precision and integer quotient rounding. Actual posted fees are already in `C`; do not subtract them again. The class-level world outcome is `Y=(Σ_i G_i)/4`, reported in USD. The **one primary contrast** is `Y_A−Y_P` at the 0.1-ABC quote scale, for each of the three development seed labels and their median/range. The 0.2-ABC scale, its contrast interaction, and M1/M2 mixed-population outcomes are secondary and explicitly low precision. There is no direction-of-success threshold and no equivalence claim. Three worlds per cell type cannot estimate a tail or support a high-powered null.

At each assigned world report all four maker gains, total maker class gain, fee postings, initial and terminal cash/base, and the two-sided terminal-mark status. If either P or A in a seed pair lacks that strict terminal mid, the paired `Y_A−Y_P` is **undefined**, not zero. Report the entire missingness pattern by arm and seed; the primary three-seed contrast is `NOT_IDENTIFIED` if any primary pair is unavailable. Available-pair differences may be shown only as conditional descriptive values, not as an unconditional treatment estimate. An empty or one-sided market with valid evidence is an economic/valuation outcome, not corrupted evidence. No last trade, seed price, stale mark, infinite-depth liquidation or survivor-only substitution is allowed. Terminal inventory remains open; `G` is a marked passive-endowment comparison, not realized liquidatable profit.

Competing explanations include inventory-policy versus spread-adaptation effects, quote priority from slot order, cap or cash/base denial, seed-once depth persisting beyond warm-up, divergent endogenous counterparty paths, and a treatment-dependent missing terminal mark. The within-roster pure/AS comparison in M1/M2 describes head-to-head outcomes but is not a unilateral best-response payoff. A negative, mixed or unavailable contrast is valid. The first falsifier of a claim that inventory-aware quoting improves economic performance is a nonpositive or unavailable registered contrast, with its observed risk/flow context preserved rather than tuned away.

## Effective environment and information

The study-specific adapter is `experiment/repeatedspot.BuildE0World`; the open world composer remains `simulations/repeatedspot.Build`. The adapter constructs the exact E0 roster from existing policy definitions. The locked plan's `effective_world` JSON is compared byte-for-byte with a fresh construction before execution. One FIFO ABC/USD spot book has base precision `10^8` atoms/ABC, quote precision `10^5` atoms/USD, a $1 tick and 0.001-ABC minimum order. Borrowing and venue automation are off. The reference $50,000/ABC locates **initial finite seed orders only**; there is no subsequent external price path or fair-value feed. A seed-once actor starts with 1 ABC and $10,000, sends one 0.1-ABC bid at $49,950 and one 0.1-ABC ask at $50,050, and neither replenishes nor is forced to disappear. Its fills, account and all-level resting depth are retained as part of the ecology.

Four maker slots each start with 50 ABC and $2.5 million, hold a ±10-ABC pending-inclusive working limit relative to initial ABC, and quote at most the assigned 0.1 or 0.2 ABC per side with a 0.001-ABC minimum. They all use the same post-only cancel/wait/replace lifecycle, 5-second decision interval, 1-second directed market-data, request and response delays, no borrowed assets and no recapitalization. The pure policy quotes a 2-bp half-spread about its delivered local mid. The AS-style policy uses the documented normalized-inventory rule, a 600-second rolling risk horizon, initial log-variance `10^-8 s^-1`, 120-second EWMA half-life, 30-second delivered-trade sample spacing, a 4× variance cap, relative risk aversion 50, relative fill decay 20,000 and one-tick minimum half-spread. At zero inventory and the initial variance/reference these rules yield approximately matched $10 half-spreads; later AS spread/variance and inventory responses are intentionally part of the package contrast. The original AS paper's Brownian mid and Poisson fill processes are **not** imposed on the matcher.

Four finite random takers each start with 20 ABC and $1 million; their 2-second policy draws direction and approximately 0.05–0.15 ABC before clipping to delivered facing depth. Two finite round-trip participants each start with 5 ABC and $250,000, evaluate every 2 seconds, may open a 0.1-ABC mandate with declared 0.05 probability when idle, and target a 5-minute holding interval. All use the same 1-second directed transport delays and a 1-bp taker fee paid in USD; maker fee/rebate is zero. Separate actor random streams are derived solely from the registered world seed and stable role slot. Random-taker draws are aligned by actor tick across arms; round-trip RNG consumption can diverge with endogenous position state. Equal seed labels therefore do **not** prove identical complete background paths or independent within-world actor replicates.

Simulation time is 55 minutes at a 1-second logical step. The fixed warm-up is `[0,10 min)` and the observation window is `[10,55 min)` for reconstructed maker filled-inventory exposure, local pending-inclusive envelope occupancy and account-attributed resting depth. No balances, orders, estimates or liabilities reset at 10 minutes. Terminal marked gain covers the **whole** world, including warm-up trading. The current `MarketSummary` and trade counts also cover the full 55 minutes; label their denominators accordingly. No wall-clock sleep models transport or computation. The exact equal-time order is the simulator's canonical event sequence, independently replayed from the E0 v3 stream.

## Finite development matrix and controls

The prospective assignments are `P`, `A`, `M1`, `M2` × maker quote quantity 0.1 or 0.2 ABC × seeds `18001`, `18011`, `18017`, in that arm/quantity/ascending-seed order: **24 economic cells**. P has four pure makers; A has four AS-style makers; M1 is pure/AS/pure/AS in slots 1–4; M2 is its AS/pure/AS/pure mirror. M1/M2 test slot/queue sensitivity, not two independent replicates. Quote size is **working execution scale**, not assigned capital or a demonstrated capital-capacity intervention: all accounts, hard working limits, demand, fees and clocks stay fixed. The seed labels were chosen prospectively after a repository metadata search found no collision with registered historical holdouts; they are development labels, not sealed confirmation seeds. Do not replace a losing seed.

The first P/0.1/18001 world is also the resource preflight: if it completes and verifies, retain it as the assigned economic cell, not an uncounted extra run. Two planned technical duplicate executions of that same cell at Go worker counts 1 and 4 test fresh-process evidence/economic determinism; they count against E0's 120-world ceiling but are **not** two more economic observations. Thus the planned maximum is 26 executions before any prospective amendment. No calibration cell is currently assigned. A resource or implementation failure remains an attempted execution and must be retained; a corrected-semantic replacement requires a new source/protocol identity and counts within the unchanged ceiling. Neither unused quota nor a quiet result licenses another arm. No historical holdout or new confirmation seed is included.

Report every assigned cell, including no-trade, partial activity, rejection, one-sided book, loss and missing mark. The primary contrast uses seed-worlds as the replication unit; multiple makers, fills and ticks within one world are correlated. Show raw per-seed values, median and observed range; do not claim a p-value, equivalence or stationarity. Secondary channel diagnostics are: exchange versus processed-local net fill exposure and squared-integral risk; pending-inclusive cap occupancy and capacity-clipped/denied *positive-target* sides; maker fills and venue rejection; all-level account resting bid/ask depth; seed depth share; full-world public spread, one-sided/empty duration and volume; and terminal wealth availability. The cap metrics do not count every policy abstention. A zero quote target, missing usable local book, waiting for cancellation and exchange balance rejection are separate states. Markout, if later added, is descriptive and cannot be relabeled causal impact.
