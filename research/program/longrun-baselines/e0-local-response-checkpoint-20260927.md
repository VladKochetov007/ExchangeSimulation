## Economic and information contract

The maker's `filled_inventory` is its **net processed fill quantity since startup**, not its total endowed base position and not the exchange account's instantaneous base balance. An exchange trade occurs at its recorded venue time; a response reaches the delayed gateway at a possibly later simulated time; the actor handles the decoded event later still. The new `gateway/maker_response_receipt` and `actor/maker_processed_response` records preserve those boundaries in one canonical E0 stream. An acceptance may release a previously buffered fill event: its exchange timestamp stays attached to the earlier execution, while its effect on local inventory starts only when processed. The replay does not classify a queued older fill as a new post-cancellation execution merely by callback order.

Replay matches delivery to the earlier exchange admission, rejection, fill, cancellation or forced-cancellation record, then matches actor processing to that delivery by request/order/trade identity, quantity, side, price, fee, remainder and simulated order. It independently accumulates signed processed fills and compares the result with each processed-response observation and every scheduled maker decision. Trade ID zero is a valid first venue trade; `1f7f13f` removed an actor-side rejection of that identity. The maker-fill integration fixture itself contains a seed fill at trade ID zero and a subsequent maker fill; a focused maker unit regression exercises the maker handling a first fill with ID zero directly.

The E0 evidence container is now `repeated-spot-opaque-v3`, epoch `0x45300003`. V1 and V2 remain historical identities; no old stream or score was rewritten. The economic policy, quote parameters, matching, fees, endowments and delay configuration are unchanged by the E0-only observers. A separate analyzer correction accepts `cancel_quotes` with no usable new target after the public book becomes one-sided; that is the expected maker lifecycle, not invalid evidence. The correction was exposed by a new filled-maker synthetic fixture. No prior E0 economic results require reanalysis because none existed.

`MakerResponseSummary.net_processed_fill_base_units` is a local-information delta. It must not be read as total base inventory or marked profit. Due-response completeness uses the declared fixed response delay as a lower bound: a canonical exchange response whose minimum delivery time is within the horizon must appear in the gateway receipt stream. A world whose actor inbox/backpressure leaves such a response unobservable is rejected for this strict E0 evidence contract, not scored as a market loss. A response genuinely still in transit at the horizon may remain unprocessed; a fill delivered before its order acceptance may be explicitly counted as buffered at the boundary.
