## What is now reconstructible

The E0 recorder uses the existing canonical `evstream` container. Its E0-specific identity is now `repeated-spot-opaque-v2`, epoch `0x45300002`, because actor outbound placement and cancellation observations were added. Version 1 remains a historical code identity; no retained historical run was rewritten. Matching, fees, policy decisions, configuration defaults, and the earlier order-only observer contract are unchanged. A new, optional cancellation observer is installed only by E0 capture. The already required public-snapshot projection remains part of the E0 world contract.

Independent replay now recomputes the fixed and AS-style maker target quotes from the recorded *processed* local book, declared policy parameters and independently replayed delivered-trade variance state. It checks every scheduled maker decision, including no-action ticks, rather than treating an absent decision as inactivity. It accepts the declared relative risk/fill-decay constants as policy beliefs, **not** as measured online fill intensities or a validation of the Avellaneda–Stoikov arrival model. The quote arithmetic is an implemented control approximation under endogenous matching; seeing a quote at that formula is mechanical evidence, not an emergent price law.

The actor-side outbound stream is joined by client/request identity and simulated time to exchange acceptance or rejection, and cancels to exchange cancellation or cancel rejection. Accepted order type, direction, size, price, post-only flag, visibility, position side, time-in-force and timestamp must match the submission. A request whose declared fixed transport window ended before the world boundary cannot silently lack a venue outcome; later sends are explicitly `unresolved_at_end`. Maker placements must match the computed target and the common post-only/size contract. Rejected attempts remain in the activity denominator. Venue-forced cancellation reasons and ordinary actor cancels are distinct; a reason string cannot be invented to bypass the actor request join.

The earlier ledger replay still checks each completed trade's two asset/cash settlement legs, two fills, fee postings and venue revenue. This successor additionally checks cumulative filled quantity against each accepted order, rejects a fill after its recorded cancellation, and catches forged cumulative quantities even when a single fill's remaining quantity looks internally plausible. The reported passive-endowment-relative gain still uses a strict two-sided terminal *public midpoint*. It is mark-based, not executable closeout profit; a genuinely one-sided synthetic world is valid evidence with `UNAVAILABLE_ONE_SIDED_OR_EMPTY` gain.

For the market series, public book deltas build the displayed book independently of snapshot messages. Each published and terminal snapshot must agree with the reconstructed top 20 levels. The compact summary partitions the exact simulated horizon into empty, bid-only, ask-only and two-sided nanoseconds, and records spread/depth integrals in fixed-point-unit × nanoseconds. Thus duplicate subscription snapshots cannot inflate elapsed market quality. Trade volume/notional are reconstructed once per accepted trade. These are descriptive market measurements, not independent observations for statistical inference or proof of stationary prices.
