# E0 PR1 implementation checkpoint — mechanical only

This checkpoint implements the first bounded milestone of the [long-run baseline plan](plan.md) on `feature/e0-repeated-spot-pr1-20260926`, based on planning revision `88717ca28ed21405debd5ec1771e114c12745be0`. It is not an executable preregistration, a development result, or permission to run the proposed 55-minute E0 cells. No new economic world or reserved seed was used.

The new [`repeatedspot`](../../../simulations/repeatedspot/world.go) package assembles one positive-price FIFO spot instrument on a simulated clock with deterministic phases. Each participant supplies its own finite base/quote endowment, fee definition, directed latency, and externally defined policy builder. The generic policy and fee definitions snapshot JSON-serializable parameters before construction, so a later mutation of the caller's maps or parameter object cannot change the exported assembly contract. `ContractJSON` returns a copy and `ContractSHA256` hashes those exact bytes. This is an **input/configuration snapshot**, not a source/config/binary/evidence attestation: arbitrary externally supplied builder closures and the Go source revision still need to be pinned by a future protocol.

`DraftE0Config` represents the planned 11-account roster: one seed-once liquidity account; four equal-endowment maker slots supplied by the caller; four recurring random takers; and two finite round-trip actors. It requires the caller to supply six random streams and all four maker policies. It does not select or reserve research seeds. The fixed-distance and AS-style spot adapters use existing actors; their parameters are exposed and encoded in the assembly contract. The draft sets one ABC/USD book, finite balances, no borrowing, one-second symmetric directed links, a one-second snapshot/step, and the provisional 55-minute horizon. These are **draft inputs**, not a locked campaign. Historical multivenue defaults are unchanged.

The new `SeedOnce` actor submits one post-only bid and ask and never replenishes, including after a fill or rejection. Its draft account has 1 ABC and $10,000; two 0.1 ABC quotes at $49,950 and $50,050 are affordable at that endowment. In the synthetic fill fixture, a separate buyer consumes the ask after the opening phase; the bid remains, the ask stays empty, and the seller's base/quote and buyer's base balances match the two-unit trade. A separate cancellation fixture checks that an ordinary actor cancel releases reserved USD without changing seed depth. These fixtures check mechanics, not the probability of natural market survival.

One timing edge matters economically. Actors started at the same simulated instant do not obtain an atomic two-order seed placement. Venue ingress can interleave another account's order between the seed bid and ask; a post-only side may then reject. The E0 draft's recurring demand begins on later decision ticks behind one-second transport, but future preregistration must retain and verify that initialization ordering. No hidden liquidity refill or post-initial external fair-price feed was added.

The small 40-second integrated fixture checks that the declared 11 actors and only one spot book are built, that existing pure and AS-style makers can receive the same simulated timer factory, and that recurring taker orders and ordinary maker fills occur. It is deliberately not a treatment/control cell; its numeric counts are fixture diagnostics, not independent economic observations. The fixture does **not** demonstrate maker superiority, survival over 55 minutes, price stability, capacity, or empirical realism.

The next economic gates remain open:

1. **E0-3:** both maker implementations currently limit filled inventory/skew, not worst-case filled plus live plus in-flight exposure. A shared quote lifecycle and pending-inclusive hard cap must be specified and tested under delayed acknowledgements, partial fills, cancel-in-flight and simultaneous orders. The synthetic roster is not a fair pure-versus-AS comparison without this.
2. **E0-4:** the AS actor uses an initial bootstrap price and a delivered-trade variance update, but the current spot adapter does not prove that the first and subsequent quotes have an available fresh local two-sided observation or a properly declared fill-exposure estimate. Its last forward can persist across a one-sided interval. Delivered-past estimator, observation/evidence joins, accounting/PnL reconstruction and fail-closed corruption tests remain necessary. The assembly contract alone cannot license a claim about information purity or net economics.

The accepted ME-001/002/003/005 development results and the R2/SV1D closure retain their original simulator/analyzer identities. This implementation is a successor technical fixture, not a rerun or revision of those findings. E2 funding/carry implementation is not started.

Validation record for this checkpoint: focused repeatedspot tests, ten repeated focused passes, package race check and package `go vet` passed. Full `make test`, repository `go vet`, and source review should be recorded after their actual completion; no acceptance is inferred from this note alone.
