This is a proposed *human-readable* extension of E0, not accepted runner configuration. It cannot be produced by merely setting `FundingIntervalSeconds` in the current multivenue builder. Any E2 source/economic change needs tests, review and a later owner-approved protocol. Source inspected at `ef61b4cb6e0124aa797f281e379ab143e847646e`.

## Funding clock and rate convention requiring a versioned implementation

Proposed N scheduled settlement instants: `t=5k min`, integer `k≥1`. Proposed S instants: `t=(1+8k) min`, integer `k≥1` (first at 9 min); this is an intentional +60-s phase. The `(20,140]` observation window contains 24 scheduled N and 15 scheduled S instants. These are calendar expectations, **not** observed successful settlements or payments. The current exchange creates its first funding tick after a full interval and may defer a missing-mark settlement; the phase adapter and exact failure/reschedule rule must be tested and pinned before interpreting counts. Do not silently treat a deferred payment as having occurred at its scheduled time.

At a scheduled settlement `t`, use only that venue's two-sided spot and perp executable-book midpoint samples at `t−60 s, …, t−1 s` as the proposed positive-domain index `I_j` and mark `M_j`. This is a *new explicit benchmark convention*, not a claim that current exchange risk marks have those sources. Proposed premium `π_j=10,000(M_j/I_j−1)` bps has 60 timestamped samples with no interpolation. Require all 60 present, fresh, positive and from that venue; otherwise rate/settlement is unavailable under a prospective fail-closed rule. At `t`, freeze the sample window and `t⁻` eligible positions after all earlier venue events; calculate and atomically post funding before processing any order/decision event stamped `t`; publish the rate and transfer evidence afterward through the normal delayed feed. Actors cannot act on that publication at the same instant. This event ordering is an **E2 semantic proposal**, not an asserted property of today's runner. Proposed per-event rate in bps:

`r_Δ = clip((1 + mean_j π_j) × Δ/(480 min), −75×Δ/(480 min), +75×Δ/(480 min))`, where `Δ=5 min` (N) or `8 min` (S).

The `1 bp` base and `75 bp` cap refer to the current *8-hour* model convention, scaled to time; they are not measured real-exchange terms. This formula is a **new compressed research contract** requiring sub-bp precision (e.g. integer micro-bps), checked arithmetic and a deterministic per-account cash remainder across payments. Current [`SimpleFundingCalc`](../../../../instrument/funding.go) uses the latest premium and integer bps; current [`settleFunding`](../../../../exchange/funding.go) posts `position value × Rate/10000` with integer truncation. The `TermCarryAllocator` also reads the current rate as integer bps, so a versioned rate unit must flow through both the exchange and policy/evidence path. Using those unchanged every 5/8 minutes would misstate economics. A strict test must reconstruct rate publication, eligible position at `t^-`, transfer sign and venue residual, cap, remainder, failed/missing mark, exact same-timestamp event order and no future premium sample. **Proposed missing-window rule:** record an unavailable scheduled payment, advance to the next fixed calendar instant and do not catch up or fabricate a transfer; the actor receives that status after its declared publication delay. This explicitly differs from the current deferral behavior and needs semantic review. A trailing 60-second window is a proposed research assumption, not equivalent to an 8-hour exchange's original averaging process. Financing/borrow is disabled in E2 Regime A; if introduced later, its per-unit-time accrual and rounding require a separate contract.

## Interventions and outcomes

Future **unapproved** matrix: carry OFF/ON × up to three development seeds = 6 economic worlds plus two technical controls. Both arms retain identical background policies, IDs, endowments, fee rules, deployments, clock phases, opening orders and idle carry slots; only permission for carry policy decisions/orders changes. RNG namespace alignment must be shown, not inferred from equal seed numbers. Venue N and S are observed components within each world, **not** independent seed replicates. No seeds are assigned now.

Primary economic object is the per-completed-term net cashflow of each carry account: costed entry spot/perp fills and fees + actual funding transfers − explicit financing (none in Regime A) + costed actual exit fills and fees. Residual spot/perp exposure and a hypothetical executable terminal closeout are separately reported; an incomplete term has **no** fabricated completed-term profit. If no term completes, the conditional primary estimate is undefined/NOT_ESTIMATED, while incidence, decision and unmatched-leg paths are valid outcomes. The OFF arm has no corresponding trade profit; use it for a **separate** basis/market-quality contrast, not an invented OFF carry cashflow.

Secondary objects: local executable basis and spread/depth/one-sided time, opportunity → delivered information → decision → admission → matched legs → payment eligibility → exit funnel; funding paid/received and net exchange residual per venue; mark age and missing state; spot/perp inventory and collateral utilization; actual borrow/debt (expected zero, verify); carry and maker wealth vs passive endowments. An observed profitable trade is not proof of synchronized prices; any basis contrast needs a predeclared OFF/ON estimator, common-randomness assessment and timing-attribution rule. Eight or more *successful* payments per venue is a readiness/coverage target, not a reason to extend a disappointing world after observing it. Missing payments are reported; no horizon rescue.

Alternative native schedule, **not selected**: N 8-hour and S 12-hour payments over roughly 96 hours of observation to see at least eight slow-venue events. This avoids minute-rate scaling but changes required market survival, resource demand and carry term. It is not a backup cell that can be selected after inspecting compressed E2 outcomes.

Acceptance requires venue-local position/mark/rate tests, sub-bp transfer arithmetic with a long-horizon remainder test, equal-time funding-vs-fill order, entry and exit after several actual payments, OFF/ON account equality, missing-mark/failed-settlement behavior and canonical independent reconstruction. R2 dated/calendar mechanics are absent from E2, and the historical P4 falsification is not overwritten by this new candidate population.
