The analyzer now rebuilds each maker's *actor-known worst-case working-inventory envelope* from its own recorded outbound placements and the exchange responses it actually processed. A sent buy placement reserves its remaining base quantity against the upper bound; a sell placement reserves against the lower bound. Admission does not release the reservation. A cancellation **request** or rejection does not release it either. Processed placement rejection, full fill, or cancellation releases the appropriate remaining quantity. A partial fill transfers quantity from reservation to filled inventory. Thus the actor-local upper/lower bounds can differ temporarily from the exchange's filled position and from the account's endowed total ABC. The replay compares both bounds and filled delta with every recorded maker decision; it checks actual new-placement quantities against the capacity available *before* that decision's sends.

These are **working-limit** metrics, not all causes of no quote: a zero target, missing local two-sided book, pending response, rejected venue admission, or finite cash/base account denial has a different interpretation.
