Both v2 rules now enter the same `RecurringMaker` actor: one delayed venue-local snapshot/trade feed, the same five-second cadence when configured equally, post-only GTC instructions, cancel-and-wait replacement, and the same pending-inclusive signed-inventory guard. For filled inventory `Q` and still-fillable buy/sell quantities `B` and `S`, the conservative working interval is `[Q−S,Q+B]`, which must remain within the configured `±L`; opposite sides do not offset one another. Sent placements reserve immediately, and sent cancellations do not release capacity. Acknowledged partial fills reduce the still-fillable amount and change `Q`; a cancellation releases only its reconciled remainder. Duplicate, wrong-side, overrun and contradictory terminal messages stop the policy and make `World.Run` return an error. A full fill before a cancel rejection is a normal race only when the fill identity and quantity already reconcile. An unresolved terminal claim at completion is invalid, not silently released risk.

Fixed v2 quotes a declared exact-basis-point distance around the latest **delivered, currently two-sided** local midpoint. The relative risk/fill-decay inputs are converted to quote-price units at the current delivered midpoint. The fill-decay value remains a **fixed belief**, not an online fitted intensity; the matcher supplies actual queueing and fills. This is an AS-derived control, not an optimal solution of the endogenous competitive book. The source [Avellaneda–Stoikov paper](https://math.nyu.edu/inmemoriam/avellaneda/HighFrequencyTrading.pdf) assumes a diffusive midprice and a stylized arrival process; neither is imposed on this exchange.
