## Intervention and estimand

The treatment is the registered addition of the finite CDF/USD supplier roster;
the matched control removes that roster. The estimand is therefore the total
population intervention effect of adding those agents, including any registered
population and scheduler-order consequences. It does not claim to isolate the
supplier policy while holding actor registration topology fixed.

For each matched fresh development seed, the primary paired metric is the
aggregate post-warm-up CDF/USD empty-side share:

`effect = control_empty_side_share - treatment_empty_side_share`

The matched effect is identifiable only when both typed survival summaries have
complete exact window coverage. A valid false survival predicate is an observed
economic outcome; a missing or malformed summary is invalid evidence.

The precommitted three-pair rule is:

* treatment is no worse than control in every pair; and
* treatment has a strictly lower aggregate empty-side share in at least two of
  the three pairs.

Absolute treatment survival, supplier-accounting validity, and this paired
effect are separate predicates. A treatment can be an absolute engineering
survival success without supporting the registered causal reduction hypothesis;
that outcome is reported as evidence-valid but mechanism-effect-not-identified.

## Anti-cheating boundary

The numerical CDF audit continues to require finite inventory/cash, delayed
local receipts, ordinary passive orders, actual fills and PnL, inventory
response, withdrawal/cancellation, zero borrowing, and concentration limits.
Those checks are necessary but are not a qualitative anti-cheating verdict.
Before freeze authorization an independent reviewer must separately assess the
retained run evidence for hidden external anchoring, mechanically guaranteed
two-sided quoting, forced replenishment, common-policy concentration, and
capacity that is effectively unlimited relative to the observed market. The
scorer reports the numerical diagnostics as provisional and retains this review
as a separate promotion artifact.

This amendment does not alter the supplier economics, the eight historical
ABC/USD suppliers, the R2 calendar, or the evidence representation. It only
closes the prospective causal/provenance boundary before a new development pair
can be launched.
