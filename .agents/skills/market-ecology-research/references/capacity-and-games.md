# Capacity, substitution and games

## Three capacities

Host capacity is runtime/RAM/disk. Execution capacity is admissible flow or
quantity. Economic capital capacity is capital employed under a specified
objective, risk and exit contract. ME-001 concerns execution quantity.

Trace assigned scale -> permitted request/risk -> actual decisions/orders ->
exposure/utilized capital -> net objective. Idle additional cash diluting a
return ratio is not market saturation. Report allocated and used capital.
Keep added capital, fixed-total redistribution, actor-count fragmentation,
competitor capital and exogenous liability/demand as different interventions.

For ecology z report the entire tested admissible set with uncertainty. Do not
force monotonicity or report only its largest point. Passing the largest point
leaves the upper boundary unknown; zero passing points is limited to the grid.
Retain losses, no-trade, forced exit, unpriceable and operationally failed cells.

## Causal ecology

Removal, restoration and resource-normalized replacement answer different
questions. Necessity is conditional on the tested ecology. A null ablation can
reflect redundancy, no opportunity or insufficient resolution.
Keep participant objectives separate: a liability hedger's utility need not
equal trading PnL. Resource normalization must include assets, debt, liabilities,
information, latency and actor count, or explicitly name what changes.

After several related studies, propose a small pinned ecology panel. A designed
fractional experiment or composition-simplex sample can limit combinatorics,
with unmeasured interactions explicit. It is not a mandatory all-strategy suite.

## Later game-theory stages

Population-conditioned payoff surfaces -> restricted best-response/regret ->
rare-policy invasion -> response graph -> explicit capital allocation/entry/exit ->
long-run stability/coexistence/cycles.

Payoff depends on the whole population, resources and institutions; market games
need not be zero-sum. Pairwise rankings do not determine arbitrary multiplayer
outcomes. Low estimated regret applies only to the sampled policy/resource class
with uncertainty; it is not proof of global Nash equilibrium.
Non-transitive invasion is not proof of persistent capital cycles.
Distinguish stationary market statistics, stable wealth shares after perturbation,
restricted strategic equilibrium, metastability and forced calendar periodicity.
Reinvestment, learning and replicator updates introduce new mechanisms and
sources/sinks; do not add them to a fixed-population experiment implicitly.
