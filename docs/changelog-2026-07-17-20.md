## Part 1 — Agent Ecology (feesim), July 17

Goal: turn the single-market fee simulation into an ecology whose price
dynamics reproduce empirical stylized facts (fat tails, volatility clustering,
super-Poisson trade arrival) rather than artifacts of the actor loop.

### `9b3662e` — Parameterize feesim; experiment log analyzer

`SimConfig` grew from a fee-rate holder into the full experiment surface:
tick-scale ratio, taker cadence, MM count/levels/refresh intervals, log-normal
actor latency (min/median/sigma), funding interval, pro-rata matcher switch.
`normalize()` fills defaults so partial JSON configs from experiment scripts
behave like `DefaultSimConfig()`. `cmd/loganalyzer` (431 lines) computes
per-run metrics from the JSONL logs: return moments, autocorrelations,
spread/depth statistics, per-client PnL attribution.

### `e6c9948` — Inventory-skew MM, state-dependent taker, stylized-fact metrics

Three mechanisms that turned out to be the load-bearing ingredients of
realistic dynamics:

- **Adaptive market maker**: per-level refresh cadence (inner levels fast,
  outer slow via staggered tick divisors), three mid-price modes (last-trade,
  book mid, microprice), and Avellaneda-Stoikov-style inventory skew — the
  reservation mid shifts against inventory so the unwind side quotes more
  aggressively. In a single-MM book the shifted quotes *are* the next mid, so
  inventory pressure becomes price impact.
- **State-dependent taker**: order side tilts toward top-of-book imbalance
  (herding), and a Hawkes-lite self-excitation term — each observed trade adds
  `ExciteAlpha` to an excitation level that decays at `ExciteBetaPerSec`; each
  tick fires `1+floor(excitation)` orders. This produced the gen-1b results:
  Fano factor 1.5 (super-Poisson), excess kurtosis 8.8 vs 5.5 baseline,
  vol-clustering ACF doubled, linear ACF ≈ 0 (Cont 2001 stylized facts).
- Log analyzer gained the corresponding metrics.

### `d392778` — Configurable runner step sleep

`RunnerConfig.StepSleep` controls the wall-clock pause after each simulated
clock advance (0 = 1µs default, negative = none). At 100µs the economy is
near-deterministic across runs (exact at 5 sim-minutes, ~0.05% divergence at
15), which removed the ±25–50% metric noise that had invalidated single-run
comparisons.

### `72cfcac`, `15c4653` — Value trader as fundamental anchor

ABIDES-style value agent: believes a static fundamental, market-buys when the
mid trades below `BandBps` under it, sells above, position-capped. This is the
mean-reversion force that pins the price level; without it the tick-floor
collapse spiral documented in the overnight campaign (price walks into the
tick floor and the book degenerates) recurs. Second commit made capital and
cadence configurable.

### `a5781c9` — Bound MM inventory skew

`SkewCapTicks` bounds the skew shift. Unbounded skew gives the mid a unit
root: every inventory round-trip leaves a permanent displacement that
compounds into a random walk of the price *level*. The cap (5 ticks in the
champion config) plus the value anchor is what made the full-realism ecology
(hawkes + herding + skew + competition) 2-hour stable — the gen-5 "A3
capped-realism" champion configuration.

### `78af848` — Tiered latency-race arbs

One extra basis arb per configured tier, each with actor latency scaled by the
tier factor, all watching the same spot/perp pair. Their relative fill shares
measure latency-race win concentration (Aquilina-Budish-O'Neill style
speed-competition experiments).

## Part 2 — Derivatives Stack, July 18

Goal: listed, expiring instruments with venue-accurate margin and settlement,
plus the trading ecology (market openers, hedging dealer, arbitrageurs) needed
for cross-market coupling experiments.

### `6bffa35` — Dated futures, European options, listing scheduler (1,591 lines)

- **`ExpiringFutures`**: cash-settled dated future embedding the perpetual's
  margin core (`Perp()` accessor lets the exchange's mark/liquidation
  machinery treat it as margined) but excluded from funding (`IsPerp() =
  false`). Settles at expiry against a TWAP of the underlying observed over a
  rolling window.
- **`EuropeanOption`**: premium-paid longs, Deribit-style seller initial
  margin `max(IMBase − OTM%, IMFloor) × S + mark premium` via the
  `OrderMarginer` interface (side-aware reservation), Black-76 marks refreshed
  by the exchange price loop, auto-exercise at expiry against the settlement
  TWAP.
- **Listing policies** (`ListingPolicy` interface): `DatedFuturesLister`
  maintains a tenor ladder aligned to a calendar grid; `OptionChainLister`
  lists call/put chains at strikes centered on spot. The exchange's expiry
  loop polls policies, announces listings/settlements on a reference-data feed
  (`_instruments`), settles expired books (cancel resting orders with exact
  ledger releases → cash-settle positions → release margin → delist).
- `price/black76.go`: premium and delta with degenerate-input collapse to
  intrinsic; `expiry.go` drives listings, mark updates, and expiries.

### `8599b50` — Race-free settlement fallback; end-to-end sim test

`settlementObserver` got its mutex (observations arrive from the exchange
price loop while settlement reads from the expiry path) and freeze-on-first-
read semantics; a full end-to-end derivatives simulation test exercises
listing → trading → expiry → conservation.

### `c8c31f1` — Venue-accurate option delivery fee

Delivery fee charged on underlying settlement notional, capped at a fraction
of exercise value (Deribit pattern: 0.015% of underlying capped at 12.5% of
option value) so worthless options settle free.

### `b941d58`, `f3b470d` — Documentation

Derivatives guide (instruments, listing policies, lifecycle feed) and the
research note on reproducible market effects.

### `2d4bc37` — Derivatives ecology (1,365 lines, `simulations/derivsim`)

- **`OptionMarketMaker`** ("the dealer"): discovers every listed contract from
  the `_instruments` feed, quotes Black-76 theo ± spread with inventory-skewed
  premiums, and **delta-hedges its aggregate book in the underlying** — the
  Frey-Stremme feedback channel: a net short-gamma dealer buys rallies and
  sells dips, amplifying underlying moves.
- **`FuturesMarketMaker`**: opens each dated future within its first 200ms.
- **`CashCarryArb`**: trades future-vs-spot basis, records the basis series by
  time-to-expiry for convergence analysis.
- **`ParityArb`**: monitors C − P = S − K per strike, executes three-leg
  conversions/reversals.
- **`OptionTaker`**: random option flow; `PBuy` controls the dealer's realized
  gamma sign.
- **`contractSet`**: shared discovery + orderID→symbol fill attribution with
  buffering for fills that arrive before their accepts (marketable orders).

Experiment results from this infrastructure (27 runs): delta-hedging feedback
confirmed 3/3 seeds (hedge flow raises underlying vol 2–6×; short-gamma
amplifies more than long-gamma, directional at n=3), and emergent put-call
parity (one arb cuts the parity gap 20–28bps → 8bps). **Both results carry the
Part-3 zombie-quote caveat below.**

### `d7bb8c5` — Untethered futures regime

Self-anchored futures quoting (each future quotes around its own last trade
instead of pegging to spot), futures added to the taker universe, √TTE-scaled
carry edge, desynchronized futures tenor. Three basis-convergence designs ran;
all inconclusive — diagnosis and gen-9 recipe recorded in EXPERIMENTS.md
(passive limit-leg arb, depth ≫ arb lot, basis measured against the running
settlement TWAP).

## Part 3 — Systematic Bug Hunt and Fixes, July 19–20

Method: manual staff-level audit of the exchange core in parallel with an
adversarial logic-hunter agent pass (spec-vs-implementation, cross-component
data flow), findings cross-validated, fixed in severity order, each fix
verified with a regression test that fails on the pre-fix code where
practical. Sixteen fixes across five commits.

### Critical: accounting and solvency

**Duplicate order IDs from liquidation** (`af60483`).
`forceClose` allocated its market-order ID as `id := e.NextOrderID;
e.NextOrderID++` while `PlaceOrder` pre-increments then uses. After any
placement, `NextOrderID` *equals* the last-used ID, so every liquidation order
duplicated the most recently placed order's ID — liquidation fills were
attributed, in logs and gateway notifications, to an innocent client's resting
order. Fixed to the pre-increment pattern; regression
`TestLiquidationOrderIDUnique` fails on pre-fix code with "liquidation order
reused resting order ID 2".

**Cross-symbol margin double-count** (`af60483`).
`CheckLiquidations` computed equity as full account cash + *this symbol's*
unrealized PnL against *this symbol's* maintenance only. A client with
positions on N symbols had the same cash counted N times: individually
healthy, globally insolvent, never liquidated. Now `buildAccountMarginProfile`
aggregates unrealized PnL, notional, and maintenance/warning requirements
across every margined book in the same quote asset (trigger symbol at the
fresh mark, others at their stored marks with reference-price and entry-price
fallbacks). Regression: two-symbol client breaching only the combined
requirement is liquidated; a single-symbol client with identical balance is
not.

**Fee revenue booked in the wrong asset** (`c38ea09`).
Clients are debited in `Fee.Asset`, but revenue was credited under the quote
asset unconditionally. With base-denominated fees (`PercentageFee{InQuote:
false}`, or any non-quote `FixedFee`) base units were destroyed on the client
side while the same number was minted into the quote revenue bucket —
per-asset conservation broken in a supported configuration that no test
covered. `recordFeeRevenue` now books each fee into its own asset;
instrument settlement paths split per-asset. Regression:
`TestBaseAssetFeeConservation` checks both BTC and USD totals.

**Hedge-mode reduce overshoot** (`af60483`, hardened in `c38ea09`).
`applyHedgePositionChange` clamps reduces at zero and silently discards the
excess — but the counterparty's fill stands, so Σ(position sizes) ≠ 0
afterward, and `settlePositionMargin` margined the phantom quantity
(`openedQty = exec.Qty − closedQty`) as if it had opened. Three-layer fix:
opened quantity now derives from the actual position delta
(`|new| − |old| + closed`, exact in all twelve sign cases including flips);
hedge-mode reducing orders larger than the position are rejected at placement
(`RejectExceedsPosition`, venue reduce-only semantics); and the validation
counts the client's *resting* reduce quantity, which makes the invariant
"resting reduce ≤ position" survive every fill — so overshoot at execution
time is impossible even with stacked orders.

**`MulDiv` 128-bit rewrite** (`c38ea09`, guarded in `230061f`).
The old `(a/c)*b + (a%c)*b/c` identity overflows when
`(qty mod precision) × price > 2^63` — with a quote precision of 1e8 (a
supported configuration) that is any price above ~$920 with fractional
quantity, producing sign-flipped notionals. Rewritten with `math/bits`
(`Mul64`/`Div64`), exact truncation toward zero for all sign combinations at
any precision, panicking if the true quotient exceeds int64 and (added in the
self-audit) on non-positive divisors, where the uint64 cast would silently
produce a wrong quotient. Verified against a `big.Int` reference.

**Zero settlement price** (`c38ea09`).
A contract whose underlying never printed settled at price 0: futures longs
were debited their entire entry notional; puts paid out the full strike.
Expiry now closes such positions flat (zero cash, zero fee) and logs
`settlement_price_unavailable`.

**Borrowing collateral compounding** (`c38ea09`).
Collateral valuation skipped negative balances and counted borrowed-in cash as
fresh collateral, so iterated borrowing converged to `factor/(1−factor) ×
equity` — 3× equity at the default 0.75 factor — and a client deep underwater
in one asset could still pledge the others at full value. Limits are now
computed against net equity (assets minus debt, negatives included), which is
invariant under borrowing itself. Regression: second max-borrow that only
passes under compounding is rejected.

### Sim-validity: the zombie-quote bug (`5ebcde4`)

The derivsim market makers never learned their own order IDs.
`optionQuotes.bidID/askID` were only ever *zeroed*; accepts were consumed by
`contractSet` for fill attribution but never routed back to quote state.
Consequently `CancelOrder(q.bidID)` never fired and the same-price
short-circuit never held: **every requote tick stacked a fresh bid/ask on top
of the stale ones, for every contract, for the whole run.** Books accumulated
zombie liquidity at historical prices — the depth the carry and parity arbs
traded against was partly phantom.

Fix: `contractSet` gained `onAccept`/`onReject` callbacks; both MMs map
request IDs to quote sides, record order IDs on accept, orphan-cancel accepts
for contracts that settled in flight, and gate requoting while a submit is
unacknowledged. White-box lifecycle tests drive the full quote → accept →
price-move → cancel + requote cycle, including the in-flight gate and its
release.

**Experiment caveat filed in EXPERIMENTS.md: all gen-8/8b/8c numbers are
qualitative until rerun.** The hedge-feedback *direction* likely survives
(hedge flow was real market orders in the underlying), but magnitudes, the
parity-gap levels, and all basis measurements were taken against contaminated
books.

### The fundamental problem: response-delivery semantics (`230061f`)

A line-by-line self-audit of the above fixes (call stack + state tracking)
surfaced the root cause behind the entire ghost-order bug family — the
randomwalk postmortem bugs, the dropped forced cancels, and a *new* hazard the
zombie-quote fix had created. One response channel had three delivery
policies:

| Response type | Old policy | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| accepts / rejects / cancel-acks | `select`/`default` — drop on full channel | Actor's in-flight state desynchronized forever; with the new MM pending-gate, a dropped accept **froze the contract's quoting permanently** |
| fills | unconditional blocking send | Hangs forever — under the exchange lock — on a gateway whose consumer stopped without closing |
| forced cancels | drop → (first fix) block | Inherited both problems in sequence |

`sendResponse` unifies all of them: fast-path send, retry with 10ms backoff
while the gateway reports running, give up once it closes. At-least-once
delivery to live actors, no permanent block under `e.mu`, clean shutdown
escape. Applied to request responses, fill notifications, both forced-cancel
paths, and `BaseMarket` dispatch. The delivery contract is recorded so
`select/default` drops never quietly return.

Also in this arc: `FundingRate` was mutated outside the exchange lock and the
*live pointer* was published into actor goroutines (torn reads); now mutated
under lock with snapshot copies published. `CancelAllClientOrders` cancels
silently no more — it sends `ForcedCancelNotification` per order like the
liquidation path. `book.ResetOrder` resets `PositionSide` on pool recycle.
Test-suite loggers were racing exchange snapshot goroutines and failing
`make test-race`; they now lock.

### Follow-up found during the five-day review (`84282af`)

**feesim adaptive MM: level freeze on quote rejection.** A rejected quote left
`levelState` with a price set and no order ID — exactly the signature the
refresh guard interprets as "accept still in flight" — so the level never
requoted again. Latent only because feesim MMs are overfunded (zero rejects in
practice); any reject would have thinned the book one level at a time,
silently. Fixed with an `EventOrderRejected` handler (regression test verified
to fail on pre-fix code). This was the **third independent instance** of the
accept/reject bookkeeping bug class in five days — randomwalk (historical),
derivsim, feesim — which argues for extracting a shared quote-lifecycle
helper.

### Smaller fixes in the hunt commits

- Market sells now check base-denominated fee headroom (`spotOrderReservation`)
  instead of bare quantity — the fee debit could drive the base balance
  negative.
- `CashCarryArb` unwinds spot hedges sized by actual fills, not by its intent
  counter (a rejected or partial leg no longer leaves residual exposure).
- `OptionChainLister` symbol collision: strikes off the quote-precision grid
  integer-divided into the same symbol and silently never listed; off-grid
  strikes now use raw-unit symbols.
- Perp/option settlement `balance_change` logs include the fee delta, so
  log-replay reconciliation no longer drifts by fees.
